Hello, world

Nov 10, 2026

Welcome to my corner of the internet. I'm starting this blog as a place to think out loud about software, side projects, and the things I learn along the way.

Why write?

Writing forces clarity. Half the bugs I've fixed were solved by explaining the problem in plain language. Publishing that thinking keeps me honest and, hopefully, helps someone else stuck on the same thing.

What to expect

  • Deep dives into projects I'm building
  • Notes on tools, languages, and workflows
  • Occasional postmortems on things that broke

No fixed schedule — just when I have something worth saying.
